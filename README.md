# FLOOD-EARLY-WARNING-SYSTEM
This system is designed to provide early warnings for potential floods by utilizing the ESP32 microcontroller, water level sensors, and notification alerts via the Telegram platform. Water level sensors are installed in channels or simulation tanks to detect changes in water elevation in real-time. Data from these sensors is transmitted to the ESP32, which processes the signals and determines the alert level based on predefined thresholds: NORMAL, CAUTION (WASPADA), WATCH (AWAS), and DANGER (BAHAYA).
